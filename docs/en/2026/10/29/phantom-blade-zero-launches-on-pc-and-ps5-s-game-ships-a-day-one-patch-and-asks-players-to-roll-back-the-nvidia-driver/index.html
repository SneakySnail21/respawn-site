Phantom Blade Zero Launches on PC and PS5: S-GAME Ships a Day-One Patch and Asks Players to Roll Back the NVIDIA Driver

The kung fu action game from Chinese studio S-GAME launched simultaneously worldwide on October 29. The developers accompanied the release with a letter from the creative director, a day-one patch and advice for GeForce owners.

FRAGLINE OFFICIAL29 October 2026
2 min read
Contents

One of the most anticipated games of the fall has reached players. Phantom Blade Zero from Beijing studio S-GAME became available on PlayStation 5 and PC (Steam and Epic Games Store) on October 29 at 02:00 UTC. The launch was simultaneous across all regions, so in North America the game went live on the evening of October 28, and in Central Europe at 3 a.m. There is no early access for buyers of the pricier editions: everyone started at the same time.

Editions, size and performance

The Standard Edition costs $59.99 in the US and the Digital Deluxe is $69.99. The expanded version adds two outfits (Wulin Legend and Blood-Steel Form), a digital art book and the soundtrack, while pre-orders came with the Treasure Basin accessory (+10% Remnant Dust drops) and the Legacy outfit, both of which can also be earned through regular play. Physical editions for PS5, including the Ultimate edition with a collectible SteelBook, are planned for 2027.

Preloading opened on October 26. According to the studio, the game takes up about 145 GB, but during the PC preload you need to free up at least 300 GB and install the game on an SSD. On PS5, Performance, Balanced and Graphics modes are available, and ray tracing can be toggled separately; PSSR is supported on PS5 Pro. By the developers’ estimate, the main story will take 20-24 hours to complete.

The creator’s letter and a warning for GeForce users

On the eve of release, creative director and S-GAME head Soulframe Liang published a long letter on Steam. He recalled that the world of Phantom Blade grew out of his student indie game Rainblood: Town of Death, made in RPG Maker twenty years ago, and that for Phantom Blade Zero the studio cancelled other projects. Thousands of hours of motion capture were recorded for the animations, masters of Guangdong lion dance and Shaolin monks were consulted, and Donnie Yen, who also played the character Mo Yuan, has served as creative consultant since 2023.

“Phantom Blade Zero exists to make you feel powerful and incredibly cool.”

Soulframe Liang, creative director and CEO of S-GAME, Steam

In the same letter, Liang listed three important updates. The day-one patch weighs about 5 GB and noticeably improves ray tracing and path tracing performance. The rasterized image has been further polished, and the game has been tested on mid-range and low-end PCs and handheld devices. Finally, the studio noticed instability on the latest NVIDIA driver 621.01 and advises rolling back to version 617.42 with a clean install until the next driver is released.

A strong start

As a reminder, the release was originally scheduled for September 9, but over the summer it was pushed to October 29 to polish the game. Judging by the first hours, the wait paid off: according to SteamDB data cited by PlayGround.ru, peak Steam concurrent players exceeded 400,000, and user reviews sit in the “Mostly Positive” range.
