AION 2: NC Weakens Expedition Bosses, Adds a Hard Mode and Bans Botters

Three weeks after launch, AION 2 received a patch that weakens bosses, two new hard dungeons, a battle pass and a wave of bans for bots and gold farming.

FRAGLINE OFFICIAL29 October 2026
2 min read
Contents

The free MMORPG AION 2 from NC came out on Steam on October 5, 2026, and in three weeks the studio has already released several updates in a row. We break down what appeared in the Steam announcements of October 27-28: expedition balance, a new hard mode, a battle pass and an account cleanup.

The October 27-28 patch: bosses get softer

The main change concerns those who get stuck in group content. In regular expeditions and on the first stage of Transcendence, boss attack is reduced by 15%. In hard expeditions and on Transcendence stages two through four, bosses have both attack and damage bonus reduced by 10%. In addition, on Transcendence stages 1 and 2, boss health is reduced by 6.5%.

At the same time, the developers added a hard mode for two locations, Urogugu Canyon and the Wakron Sky Island. Entry requires an item level of 2700, the clear is limited by a four-minute “Rage” timer, and no more than three resurrections are allowed.

Battle pass and events

Along with the patch, players get cosmetics too: a separate notice from October 28 points to the Daeva Lookbook catalog with new skins.

From October 28 to December 2, the Daeva Pass: Prelude to Flight is available. The basic version is free, the premium one costs 1000 Kuna and includes 20 bound enhancement scrolls. The pass’s maximum level is 50, extra levels are sold for 50 Kuna each, and pass experience is capped at 15,000 per character per week.

  • the 28-day “Call of Atreia” event with daily rewards runs from October 28 to early December;
  • Halloween Twitch drops are active until November 3-4: an hour of watching gives 15 resurrection scrolls, and eight hours gives the “Chubby Panda” helmet;
  • earned drops must be claimed by November 17-18.

Bans for bots and gold farming

In a separate announcement, NC said it had restricted accounts caught using macros, bots and third-party programs, as well as organized gold farming and real-money trading. In addition, some accounts were temporarily blocked over possible security risks; as clarified, this is not a punishment, and owners are invited to contact support. Sanctions are applied only after review, and you can file an appeal through a ticket.

“Regular play, including long grind sessions, is not affected.”

The AION 2 team, AION 2 Steam news

On the eve of the patch, the servers were closed for scheduled maintenance for about four and a half hours, and Steam also published an Abyss server matchmaking table by region: server pairs, for example Triniel versus Lumiel in Europe, will be updated periodically.

For a game that lives off its in-game economy, such cleanups are an expected step: together with the boss softening, they show that NC is working on both the entry threshold and the fairness of the market. The game runs on Windows only and is free to play.
