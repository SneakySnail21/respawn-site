Phantom start: Phantom Blade Zero draws up to 775,000 players on Steam

FRAGLINE OFFICIAL7 hours ago
2 min read
Contents

The Chinese action game Phantom Blade Zero launched on October 28-29 and immediately blew up the top of Steam. In the first hours, concurrent players topped 400,000, and by the weekend the record had climbed to 775,000. For a paid single-player game, those are serious numbers, almost on par with free-to-play hits.

What the game is

Phantom Blade Zero is an action RPG in a kung fu punk style. You play a warrior named Soul, who has been framed and left with a wound in his heart. He has 66 days to find out who is behind it and make his way through a dark wuxia world with weapons you can swap on the fly. Combat is fast, brutal and very spectacular, like a good martial arts movie.

The game is out on PC (Steam and other stores) and PlayStation 5. A physical PS5 edition will arrive in 2027. The price is standard for AAA: around $60. Before release, analysts spoke of roughly 850,000 pre-orders, and after launch the figure kept growing. Most of the sales came from China and neighboring regions.

How the reviews look

Critics have received the game warmly: the average score is around 88. Players on Steam are mostly positive too, at around 76-79%. Many compare it to Black Myth: Wukong, which is understandable: both projects come from China and both were made on a grand scale. But Phantom Blade Zero is not a copy; it has its own story with an emphasis on melee combat and the style of kung fu cinema.

There were minor technical problems with NVIDIA drivers at launch, but the developers rolled out a patch right away. Concurrent players fluctuate, high during the day and dropping at night, which points to a strong Asian audience.

Why it matters

The game’s success shows that big single-player projects from China are no longer a rarity. If you like fast fights, beautiful locations and a story with a conspiracy, it is worth a look. Especially if you love martial arts movies and want something similar in a game.

For now the game is fresh and player counts are high. If you are thinking of trying it, now is the best time, while everyone is hotly discussing it and sharing patches. And if you are not sure, watch the trailer and a couple of streams to see whether the combat style clicks with you.
